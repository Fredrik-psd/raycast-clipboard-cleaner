# Clipboard Cleaner Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

### Cleaners
- **Remove Empty Lines**, **Trim Whitespace**, **Collapse Blank Lines**, **Sort Lines**, **Dedupe Lines**.
- **Lowercase**, **UPPERCASE**, **Title Case**, **Sentence case**.
- **Strip Tracking Parameters** (`utm_*`, `fbclid`, `gclid`, …) from URLs in the clipboard.
- **Straighten Smart Quotes**, **URL Encode**, **URL Decode**, **Pretty-Print JSON**, **Minify JSON**.
- **Add Space** and **Replace Text**, each with a live preview of the cleaned result.

### Commands
- **Clipboard Cleaner** picker with every cleaner.
- **Paste as Plain Text** strips rich-text formatting from the clipboard.
- **Restore Previous Clipboard** undoes the most recent cleaner.
